One package, three storefronts.

$40,000one-time · you own all three stores

Three revenue channels, one operator. Amazon and Walmart storefronts for reach, plus a store on Rarely, Stable's own platform, where Stable controls the supply and the marketplace directly.

  • You own the storefronts and the seller accounts. Stable operates them for you.
  • Stable builds, stocks, and runs everything — sourcing, listings, fulfillment, account health.
  • Inventory is separate and is the growth lever (next page).
  • Hands-off for you. Stable's team does the work; you review the numbers.

Why three stores instead of one

Amazon and Walmart are the two largest marketplaces in the country, and each comes with its own rules. Account reviews and policy actions happen to good sellers. A single marketplace store is a single point of failure.

Rarely is the platform Stable owns and controls. It runs on Stable's inventory, so it isn't exposed to a third party's policy decisions. Pairing all three gives you diversification inside one package.

This is the package Stable recommends and the one we present.

Expectations
What to expect — honestly

A real business, on a real timeline.

Nobody here promises you a number. What we can tell you is how a store typically develops, based on how Stable's partner stores have behaved. Give it a quarter to build. Judge it over a year.

Months 1–3

Build-out

Entity, accounts, listings, first inventory. Expect little to no return in this window — the store is being built.

Month 4+

Ramp

Stores are live and inventory is cycling. Sales climb toward run-rate; don't expect the full run-rate in month four either.

Operational

10% true-up rebate

On Amazon, you make at least 10% on every item sold in your store, every month. If a month falls short, Stable discounts your next inventory order by the shortfall.

Year one

20–25% target

Stable's blended first-year target across the package. Rarely stores target roughly 10% per 90-day inventory cycle; Amazon typically runs lower in year one. Blended, the package lands in this band. More inventory has meant materially better results.

How the true-up works (Amazon store)

Say your store sells $10,000 of inventory in a month and returns $10,700 — a 7% margin. Stable credits the $300 shortfall as a straight discount on your next inventory order, truing you up to 10% profit for that month. The floor is on the profit you make on inventory sold; inventory sizing still drives the total dollars.

True-up rebate applies to the Amazon storefront and is credited against your next inventory purchase; full terms are in your Stable agreement. First-year target is not a guarantee. E-commerce carries risk, including loss of capital. Informational only; not an offer of securities.

Straight Answers
The questions everyone asks

We'd rather you ask now than wonder later.

Do I actually own the stores?

Yes. The storefronts, the seller accounts, and the inventory are yours. Stable operates them on your behalf.

How much of my time does this take?

Very little. Stable's team handles sourcing, listings, fulfillment, and account health; you review the numbers and stay informed on your stores.

What does the $40,000 cover, and what doesn't it?

It covers building and operating all three storefronts. Inventory is separate and is your capital — in product you own. Any ongoing fees are walked through line by line on your call before you sign anything.

What if Amazon suspends the account?

It happens to legitimate sellers, which is exactly why the package includes a Rarely store on Stable's own platform. Stable's team handles Amazon account health and compliance day to day.

Who is Exelion, and who is Stable?

Exelion Group is your introduction and point of contact; we work with several e-commerce partners. Stable Storefronts is our preferred vendor for Amazon, Walmart, and Rarely — the operator that actually runs your stores.

Can I talk to someone who's done this?

Yes — it's built into the process. Your second conversation is with a current Stable partner who owns stores in the program.

What returns should I expect?

Plan on a 90-day build. Once operational, Stable's 10% true-up means you make at least 10% on inventory sold through your Amazon store each month, with any shortfall credited to your next order. Stable's blended first-year target on the package is 20–25% — Rarely targets roughly 10% per 90-day cycle, Amazon runs lower in year one, and the blend lands in that band. Inventory drives the total.

What exactly am I signing?
